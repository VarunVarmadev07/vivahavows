About Me

Founder & Lead Photographer, Vivaha Vows Studio


Hi, I’m Vamshi — the creative mind and heart behind Vivaha Vows Studio, based in Hyderabad. My journey in photography began in 2019, sparked by curiosity and a deep desire to capture genuine emotions. What started as a simple interest soon transformed into a meaningful purpose: to create photographs that don’t just look beautiful, but feel alive, personal, and unforgettable.

To build a strong foundation, I completed a two-year professional photography internship, where I gained hands-on experience in lighting, composition, and visual storytelling. More importantly, I learned to observe emotions, understand expressions, and capture real moments as they naturally unfold. This training shaped my approach and helped me grow into a confident photographer who believes every frame should tell a story.

Today, I work as a full-time professional photographer, dedicated to preserving memories that matter most. Through Vivaha Vows Studio, I have had the privilege of covering weddings, events, portraits, and creative projects—each one unique, each one meaningful. My goal is simple: to document your moments with authenticity, warmth, and artistry, so they can be cherished for a lifetime.
